Get support

Asking for help is a strong, sensible step. You do not need to wait until things feel unbearable.

If you are in immediate danger, call 112 (India's emergency number). For free, 24/7 mental-health support in India, call Tele-MANAS on 14416. Please verify numbers on the official government site before relying on them, as they can change. Outside India, findahelpline.com lists local lines.

When should I consider asking for help?

  • Difficult feelings last for weeks, not days.
  • They get in the way of study, sleep, eating or relationships.
  • You feel unable to cope on your own.
  • You feel hopeless, or have thoughts of harming yourself.

Who you can talk to

A trusted friend, a parent or guardian, a teacher, your college counsellor, or a qualified mental-health professional such as a psychologist or psychiatrist.

Helping a friend

Listen without judging. Take their worries seriously. Don't promise to keep danger secret. Encourage them to reach out, and offer to go with them.

For parents and teachers

Students often juggle academic and social pressure and uncertainty about the future. Listen first, avoid quick judgement, keep routines healthy, and help connect them with support.

In our survey, 22 of 74 students didn't know where to get help and 10 weren't sure. If that's you, this page is a good place to start.
