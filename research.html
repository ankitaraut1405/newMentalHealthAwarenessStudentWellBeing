What the findings mean

Awareness is high, but access to help is not

Most students want more awareness, yet many don't know or aren't sure where to get help. Clear signposting, like the Get Support page, matters.

Academic pressure moves with feeling overwhelmed and tired

Pressure, overwhelm and emotional tiredness are strongly linked. Workload, planning and exam support are likely to help.

Knowing where to get help goes with willingness to talk

Chi-square p < 0.001. 34 of 42 students who know where to get help would talk to someone.

Fear of judgement is the biggest barrier

It is the most common reason students give for not talking. Private, anonymous and friendly options may lower this barrier.

Lifestyle shows weaker links

Exercise shows a weak link with feeling overwhelmed, while sleep and screen time show little. Broad answer bands limit what can be detected.

Python analysis workflow

  1. Collect: Google Form, anonymous and voluntary
  2. Export: CSV dataset (74 responses, 22 questions)
  3. Clean (Pandas): tidy column names, standardise age groups, split multi-select answers
  4. Analyse: counts, percentages, cross-tabs, Spearman correlation, chi-square
  5. Visualise: Matplotlib in the notebook; interactive charts here
  6. Report: findings, limits and recommendations

Method and limitations

  • The sample is small (74), self-selected and mostly from two year groups, so it does not represent all students.
  • Answers are self-reported and are not a clinical measure.
  • Data check needed: the "supported" rating moves in the same direction as feeling overwhelmed and tired, which is unexpected. This may mean the scale labels were read the other way round, so it is not interpreted as a finding.
  • Most open-text answers were blank, so they are not analysed here.
  • With small filtered groups, results can change a lot from one student.

Privacy

The survey was anonymous and used only for this academic project. The check-in, quiz, Thought Space and Guide on this site are separate from the dataset. Saved entries stay in the visitor's own browser.
