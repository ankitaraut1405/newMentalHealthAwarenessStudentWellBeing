    Starting a hard conversation

    Sample sentences you can adapt for a parent, teacher or friend.

    To a parent

    "I've been feeling under a lot of pressure lately. Can we talk when you have a few minutes?"

    To a teacher or counsellor

    To a friend

    "I haven't felt like myself. Could I tell you what's been going on?"
