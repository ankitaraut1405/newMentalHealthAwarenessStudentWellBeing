# 🌿 Mind & Well — Student Mental Health & Well-Being

**Mind & Well** is a student-focused mental health and well-being website created as a **BSc Data Science project**.

The project combines **student survey analysis, mental health awareness, interactive activities, reflection tools, relaxation exercises, educational resources, and a Python-based wellbeing chat system** in one simple and student-friendly platform.

---

## 🌱 About the Project

Students can experience academic pressure, stress, loneliness, sleep problems, relationship difficulties, and other challenges during college life.

Mind & Well was created to provide students with a safe and easy-to-understand place where they can:

* Learn about mental health
* Understand common emotional experiences
* Explore simple wellbeing activities
* Relax through calming exercises
* Reflect on their thoughts
* Find books, music, and creative activities
* Learn how to start difficult conversations
* Find reliable support resources
* Explore findings from a student wellbeing survey
* Interact with a Python-based wellbeing chat system

> **The goal is not to diagnose mental health conditions. The website provides awareness, general wellbeing information, and guidance on finding appropriate support.**

---

# 🎯 Project Objectives

1. Increase awareness about student mental health.
2. Help students understand common emotional and wellbeing challenges.
3. Provide simple activities that can help students pause, relax, and reset.
4. Encourage healthy communication and reaching out for support.
5. Provide useful resources for students, parents, and educators.
6. Analyse student survey data using Data Science techniques.
7. Connect the survey findings with a practical student-focused website.
8. Demonstrate how Python and web technologies can be used to build a real-world student wellbeing project.

# 🔐 Privacy

The project is designed with student privacy in mind.

* No personal student identity is displayed.
* Survey information is used for academic analysis.
* Reflection notes are stored locally in the browser where applicable.
* The website does not claim to diagnose mental health conditions.
* Users are encouraged to seek qualified professional help when needed.
